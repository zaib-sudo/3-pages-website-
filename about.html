WHO WE ARE

Small steps. Big journeys.

StepUp Shoes is a fictional shoe-store brand created for an HTML and CSS assignment. Our concept is simple: shoes should look good, feel comfortable, and suit different moments in everyday life.

This website demonstrates a three-page layout, shared navigation, headings, paragraphs, links, product cards, and responsive CSS styling.

Our mission

To make it easy for everyone to discover a style that feels right for them.
